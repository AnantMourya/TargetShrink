Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. File Processing Architecture (Your Files Never Leave Your Device)

TargetCompress (accessible at targetcompress.in) operates strictly using client-side WebAssembly (Wasm) and HTML5 technologies (including FFmpeg.wasm, PDF-Lib, and Canvas). File processing happens directly in your browser. Your videos, images, PDFs, and documents are never uploaded, transmitted, stored, or analyzed on any external server or cloud backend.

2. Advertising & Google AdSense Compliance

TargetCompress is supported through digital advertisements to maintain all compression and document conversion tools 100% free without charging subscription fees or requiring user account registrations. We adhere strictly to Google Publisher Policies:

  • Third-Party Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to TargetCompress or other websites across the internet.
  • Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant advertisements to users based on their browsing behavior on TargetCompress and other internet destinations.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice page at www.aboutads.info/choices or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at optout.networkadvertising.org.

3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device. TargetCompress utilizes cookies and local storage strictly for:

  • Essential Preferences: Remembering your selected theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode) via local storage key tc-theme. This preference contains zero personally identifying information.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregated, anonymous operational metrics via Vercel Speed Insights and Web Vitals (DNS resolution time, First Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift) to maintain platform speed and prevent downtime.
  • Advertising: Third-party cookies set by Google AdSense to prevent repetitive ad displays and report aggregate ad performance.

4. International Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

We respect user privacy across all jurisdictions:

  • European Union (GDPR): Under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation EU 2016/679), European visitors have the right to access, rectify, or erase any personal data held. Because TargetCompress does not collect names, email addresses, or store files on servers, we hold no personal data dossiers on our users.
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California consumers have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. TargetCompress does not sell or rent user personal information.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TargetCompress does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such data from our records.
