## 2. Detailed Description

```markdown
TargetCompress lets you compress images and documents to your exact target file size (e.g., 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, 1 MB) directly in your browser with zero cloud uploads.

Never get rejected by government job portals, admission forms, or email upload limits again.

⚡ KEY FEATURES

• Exact Target Size Control: Specify your exact target limit in KB or MB. Our mathematical bisection algorithm automatically tunes compression parameters to hit your target on the first try.
• 1-Click Instant Presets:
  - 50 KB: Standard Indian Government / SSC / UPSC photo forms
  - 20 KB: Official applicant signature uploads
  - 100 KB: State PSC, bank clerk, and entrance examination documents
  - 1 MB: Web publishing and fast mobile loading
• 100% Private & Client-Side: Your files are processed entirely in your local browser memory using modern HTML5 Canvas. Media files are NEVER uploaded to any remote server or third party.
• Right-Click Image Shortcut: Right-click any image while browsing to immediately compress it.
• Instant Download: Fast one-click download directly into your default downloads folder.

💡 HOW TO USE

1. Click the TargetCompress icon in your browser toolbar.
2. Choose a preset (50 KB, 20 KB, 100 KB) or type your custom target size.
3. Drop your image (JPG, PNG, WebP) or click to browse.
4. Download your compressed file instantly!

Need to merge PDFs, split documents, or compress video? Access our full suite of free browser tools anytime at https://www.targetcompress.in/
```

- **Does this extension collect user data?** **NO.**
- **Does this extension transmit user data to external servers?** **NO.** All compression runs locally on client-side CPU buffers.
