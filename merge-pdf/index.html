How to Merge PDF Files Online in Your Browser

  1. Select or Drop PDFs: Select two or more PDF documents from your computer or phone. All files remain strictly on your local device.
  2. Arrange Document Order: Reorder your files into the exact sequence you want them combined in the merged document.
  3. Merge PDFs: Click Merge PDFs Now. The in-browser engine stitches document streams together locally without uploading anything.
  4. Download Merged PDF: Click Download Merged PDF to save your combined document immediately.

Useful Optimization Tips for Merge PDF Files Online

  • Drag and drop documents to arrange them in the exact order you want them combined.
  • Great for combining resume, cover letter, and certificates into a single job application PDF.
  • Once merged, use our PDF Compressor to shrink the combined file under portal ceilings like 500KB or 1MB.
