How to Compress PDF to 100KB for Govt & Job Portals

📅 September 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read 🔒 Verified 100% In-Browser

Reducing a scanned PDF document to under 100KB is one of the most frustrating challenges applicants face when submitting online government forms, university applications, and visa packets. Learn how our browser-side PDF compressor achieves sub-100KB sizes while keeping text razor-sharp.

Why PDFs Balloon in Size

A standard 1-page PDF document created by modern scanners or smartphone scanning apps frequently weighs between 2MB and 8MB. Why is a single page of text so large? Three hidden elements cause this inflation:

  • Uncompressed Scanner Color Depths: Scanner apps capture at 300 to 600 DPI in 24-bit full RGB color, even for simple black-and-white documents.
  • Embedded Font Outlines: Full subsets of proprietary fonts are bundled into the PDF header.
  • Duplicate Object Metadata: Thumbnail previews and XML document history streams add hidden kilobytes.

The Secret to Clean 100KB PDF Compression

Traditional tools crush the entire PDF raster quality indiscriminately, making letters blurry and illegible. TargetCompress uses an intelligent stream pipeline:

  1. Text Stream Isolation: True vector typography is preserved whenever possible so letters stay crisp at any zoom level.
  2. Adaptive Image Downsampling: High-resolution embedded photographs or stamps are downsampled to 150 DPI—the perfect balance between file size and readability.
  3. Stream Deflation: Redundant object dictionaries and unreferenced font subsets are stripped cleanly.
