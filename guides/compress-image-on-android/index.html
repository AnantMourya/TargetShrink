How to Compress Images to 50KB on Android

📅 September 2026 ⏱️ 3 min read 🔒 Verified 100% In-Browser

Need to compress a camera photo or document scan to under 50KB or 100KB on your Android phone without downloading shady, ad-bloated apps from the Google Play Store? Here is the fast, safe, browser-based method.

Why You Shouldn't Download Random Android Compressor Apps

Most free photo compressor apps in the app store demand invasive permissions (access to your contact list, location, and entire photo gallery), spam you with full-screen video ads, and upload your personal photos to unknown overseas servers. TargetCompress runs 100% locally inside Google Chrome or Samsung Internet with zero permissions required.

3-Step Android Compression Guide

  1. Open Google Chrome or your default browser on your Android device.
  2. Visit targetcompress.in/compress-image-to-50kb.
  3. Tap Browse Local Image and select the photo from your Gallery or Google Photos.
  4. Tap Compress to Exact Target Size. The browser processes the image in less than one second.
  5. Tap Download Compressed Image. The optimized photo is saved directly into your phone's Download folder, ready to be attached to your online form.
