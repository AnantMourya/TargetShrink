How to Compress Scanned Signature to 20KB

📅 September 2026 ⏱️ 3 min read 🔒 Verified 100% In-Browser

Digital signature upload rejections are among the most common errors encountered on government job, banking, and entrance exam portals. Portals typically mandate that signatures be strictly under 20 KB with clear black or blue ink on an unshadowed white background.

Why Signatures Get Rejected by Online Validators

  • File Exceeds 20KB: A raw smartphone camera photo of a signature on paper usually weighs between 2MB and 4MB—more than 100 times the allowable limit.
  • Grey or Shadowy Background: Taking a photo indoors casts phone shadows on the paper, making the background grey instead of white.
  • Wrong Aspect Ratio: Upload validators expect rectangular dimensions like 140×60 pixels or 200×100 pixels.

Step-by-Step: Preparing a Perfect 20KB Signature

  1. Step 1: Sign on Clean White Paper: Use a black or dark blue ballpoint/gel pen on unruled, pure white paper in good lighting.
  2. Step 2: Crop Closely: Before uploading, crop away 90% of the surrounding white page. Keep only 5-10mm of margin around your signature strokes.

  4. Step 4: Automatic Contrast & Compression: The tool optimizes the image to 20KB while maintaining crisp, unbroken pen strokes.
  5. Step 5: Download: Save the file and upload with confidence to your application form.
