How to Split PDF & Extract Pages in Your Browser

  1. Select or Drop PDF: Drag your PDF document into the drop zone. The file is read entirely inside your browser sandbox.
  2. Specify Page Ranges: Enter individual page numbers (e.g. 1, 4) or page ranges (e.g. 2-5) to extract, or select all pages.
  3. Extract Selected Pages: Click Extract Selected Pages. The client-side parser isolates your chosen pages into a clean new document.
  4. Download Extracted PDF: Click Download Extracted PDF to save your separated pages or ZIP archive immediately.

Useful Optimization Tips for Split PDF & Extract Pages

  • Specify individual pages separated by commas (e.g. 1, 4, 7) or ranges separated by hyphens (e.g. 2-5).
  • Extracting just the required certificate page from a huge 50-page marksheet bundle drastically lowers file size.
  • Client-side processing ensures sensitive financial and medical documents are never exposed to third-party servers.
